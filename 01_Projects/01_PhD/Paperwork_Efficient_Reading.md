- **Read three times**:
	- 1st (10 - 20% of the time): just a **general view** of the book/paper.
	- 2nd (20 - 80%): detailed knowledge in take, in book/paper **note taking** and highlighting.
	- 3rd (20 - 30%): **notes destilation**.
- **3x1h reading** better than 3h reading.
**Source:** [Link][https://pne.people.si.umich.edu/PDF/howtoread.pdf]
